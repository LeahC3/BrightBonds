Bright Bonds

A RISE project for the Bishop Verot Scholars' Academy, connecting high school students with senior citizens at Shell Point Retirement Community.

The Project

Bright Bonds is a program that pairs high school students with residents at Shell Point Retirement Community based on shared interests. Savannah Emrich and I (Leah Chung) co-founded it as our RISE (Capstone) project for the Bishop Verot Scholars' Academy. We aimed to match students up with senior citizens by shared interests and have them meet regularly over time. In creating the program, we wanted to produce something more impactful than a typical one-time volunteer event.

Planning began in January 2025, with ongoing sessions launched at Shell Point in November. Individual matching took longer than we originally anticipated, but we eventually established 5 pairs, and we intend to continue the program next year.
