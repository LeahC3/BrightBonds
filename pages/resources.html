Program Overview

Bright Bonds is a volunteer-based program in SWFL that connects high school students with nursing home residents for companionship through in-person visits and messages sent securely through our website.

Guidelines for Participation

For Students:

  • Respect and kindness: Treat all seniors with patience and empathy.
  • Commitment: Attend scheduled visits and maintain regular communication.
  • Privacy: Do not ask for or share personal contact information (home address, phone number, etc.).
  • Safety: Follow all nursing home rules and remain in designated areas.
  • Professionalism: Dress appropriately and engage in meaningful conversations.
  • Appropriate topics: Avoid sensitive topics (politics, religion, etc.) unless initiated by the senior.
  • Supervision: Always follow the facility's guidelines regarding staff supervision.
  • Open to all high school students: Students from any school are welcome to participate in the program. However, participants are responsible for providing their own transportation to and from the nursing home for in-person visits.

For Seniors/Staff:

  • Encouragement: Help residents feel comfortable with student interactions.
  • Supervision: Ensure that visits occur in safe, designated areas.
  • Communication support: Assist residents with messages if needed.
  • Feedback: Share any concerns or suggestions to improve the program.

Communication/Scheduling

  • Messages: All electronic communication should go through the website.
  • Visits: Must be scheduled using the program's calendar tool and approved by nursing home staff.

Code of Conduct and Safety Measures

  • No inappropriate language, gestures, or behaviors.
  • No requests for money, gifts, or personal favors.
  • If any concerns arise, report them to program organizers immediately.

Volunteer Hours

Students will receive service hours for in-person visits (tracked by the nursing home staff and MobileServe app).

How to Report Issues

If any issues arise (inappropriate behavior, safety concerns, scheduling problems), contact the program coordinator at Savannah.Emrich@bvhs.org or DM our instagram at @brightbonds_swfl.

Connection Library

Download Activity Library

Whether you're just starting your match or have been visiting for months, our Connection Library is full of thoughtful prompts and simple activities to make every conversation easier, warmer, and more fun.

Conversation Starters

  • What's a food you wish you liked but just don't?
  • Did you ever have a silly nickname growing up?
  • What's your dream vacation destination? Or what was your favorite vacation destination?
  • If you could instantly master any skill, what would it be?
  • What's the best advice you've ever received?
  • What life moment changed you the most?
  • If you could relive one day in your life, which would it be and why?
  • What's something you're really proud of?
  • How was high school different when you were my age?
  • What's one invention you wish had existed back then?
  • What kinds of music or dances were popular when you were younger?
  • Coffee or tea?
  • Mornings or nights?
  • Cats or dogs?
  • Books or movies?

Simple Activities

Message Based:

  • Share a joke/riddle of the week
  • Ask each other to describe a favorite photo and why it matters
  • Take turns recommending a song or poem

In-Person:

  • Bring a simple game (Uno, cards, dominoes)
  • Create a playlist together based on shared music tastes
  • Make a mini time capsule
  • Try coloring or folding origami
  • Do a "Would You Rather" round with a few silly/thoughtful questions
  • Watch a short video or listen to a podcast episode and discuss it

Themed Prompts

Fall/Halloween:

  • Did you dress up for Halloween as a kid? What was your favorite costume?
  • What's your favorite fall food or recipe?
  • Are you more of a pumpkin or apple cider fan?

Winter:

  • Do you have a favorite holiday tradition?
  • What's the best gift you've ever received or given?
  • What was winter like where you grew up?
