Get Involved

Join Bright Bonds as a student volunteer, follow along on Instagram, or bring the program to your community.

Volunteer as a Student

We're looking for student participants for the 2026–2027 school year. Sign up on the Bright Bonds platform to fill out an interest form and get matched with a Shell Point resident who shares your hobbies and interests. Matches meet regularly for visits.

Signing up takes a few minutes, and we'll reach out when sessions start next year.

Sign Up at BrightBonds.org

Follow Us on Instagram

We're on Instagram at @brightbonds_swfl. Follow along for photos and updates from our sessions, and send us a DM with any questions or feedback!

Visit @brightbonds_swfl

Bring Bright Bonds to Your Community

We're currently at Shell Point Retirement Community, and we're looking to expand. If you're a student, educator, or retirement community coordinator interested in launching this program elsewhere, we'd love to hear from you.
