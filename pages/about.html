About Bright Bonds

What it is, why we started it, and who's involved.

The Problem

Many senior citizens, particularly those in retirement communities, go extended periods without regular visitors or meaningful conversation. There's no easy way for people outside those communities to form ongoing connections with the people inside them. Also, most high school volunteering involves a single event, a few hours, and then nothing sustained.

Our idea came from college roommate programs, where incoming students fill out forms about their lifestyle and interests and get paired with someone compatible. We wanted to apply similar logic here: have students and residents fill out interest forms, then match pairs who could engage in shared hobbies or have something unique to talk about. The goal was to create something closer to a real relationship than a routine volunteer obligation. Students learn from different perspectives and residents provide mentorship or share their experiences.

The Program

📍

Where

Shell Point Retirement Community in Fort Myers, Florida. It's one of the largest continuing care retirement communities in the country.

🗓️

When

We started planning in January 2025 and held our first sessions in November 2025. We spent the first several months running group sessions to build familiarity and identify participants who could commit to a regular schedule before moving to individual pairs. The program is ongoing, and we plan to continue in the 2026–2027 school year.

💛

How

Students and residents each fill out an interest form when they join. An algorithm on the Bright Bonds website uses those responses to automatically pair them with someone who shares their interests. Once matched, pairs can communicate through the site's messaging system and coordinate visits using a shared calendar.

📖

Support Materials

We compiled a volunteer handbook and a library of activities and conversation starters for volunteers to use during visits. Both were developed during the planning phase starting in January 2025.

Who's Behind It

Bright Bonds was co-founded by two Bishop Verot Scholars' Academy students as their RISE project.

Leah Chung

Co-Founder

Developed the website and assisted with planning. Built the full Bright Bonds web platform, brightbonds.org, which includes an interest-based matching algorithm, a messaging system, a shared calendar, and user authentication. The site is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend; AWS Lambda functions handling the backend; DynamoDB for data storage; and AWS Cognito for authentication. The matching algorithm pairs students and residents based on shared interests. The platform placed runner-up in the FL-19 Congressional App Challenge.

Savannah Emrich

Co-Founder

Directed logistics, outreach, and communication. She established the partnership with Shell Point, performed much of the planning that got the program running, and organized the sessions and promotion events throughout. She also manages the Bright Bonds Instagram (@brightbonds_swfl), which documents and promotes the project.

A RISE Project

Bright Bonds is a RISE project through the Bishop Verot Scholars' Academy. The RISE project is a take-action service opportunity that asks students to find a problem, research it, and build something that addresses it meaningfully.
