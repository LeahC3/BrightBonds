# BrightBonds Showcase

This branch is dedicated to showcasing the BrightBonds RISE project for the Scholars' Academy at Bishop Verot High School.

## About the Project

BrightBonds is an intergenerational connection program co-founded as a RISE project. The goal was to match high school volunteers with seniors at Shell Point Retirement Community based on shared interests, fostering meaningful relationships across age groups.

## What We Did

- Co-founded BrightBonds with Savannah — I focused on building the website and project planning; Savannah handled logistics, communication, and outreach
- Planning began January 2025; program launched November 2025 and is ongoing
- Met 14 times for one hour each at Shell Point Retirement Community
- Held group sessions while recruiting residents and students who could commit regularly, rather than jumping straight into individual matching
- Created 5 matches after working through early challenges with scheduling and attracting consistent participants
- Produced a volunteer handbook and a library of activities/conversation starters used in the program
- Built the BrightBonds web platform, which earned runner-up in the FL-19 district (Rep. Byron Donalds) Congressional App Challenge
- Invited to a meet and greet with Congressman Byron Donalds as a result

## RISE Rubric Themes to Cover

Based on `WHYDOACAPSTONE.md`, the site should demonstrate:
- **Problem solving** — identifying the issue of social isolation and designing a solution
- **Genuine service** — other-oriented motivation, not checkbox volunteering
- **Sustained dedication** — 14 sessions over time, long-term commitment
- **Community impact** — real matches made, real relationships formed
- **Growth and evolution** — adapting the approach when early plans didn't work out
- **Teamwork** — collaboration between co-founders with distinct roles
